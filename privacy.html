Privacy Policy — Gull Bomber

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy describes what data Gull Bomber ("the App") collects and how it's used.

What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. Gull Bomber currently stores all game data — your best score, coins earned, unlocked skins, and your selected settings — locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us or any third party.

The App does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. There is no account creation, no sign-in, and no analytics tracking of any kind in the current version.

Advertising

The current version of Gull Bomber does not display ads and does not include any advertising SDK.

Third-party services

The current version of Gull Bomber does not integrate any third-party data or analytics services.

Children's privacy

Gull Bomber does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future update adds features that change what data is collected, this policy will be updated first, describing clearly what's collected and why.
