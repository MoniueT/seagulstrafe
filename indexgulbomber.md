# Privacy Policy — Gull Bomber

_Last updated: [FILL IN DATE WHEN YOU PUBLISH THIS]_

This policy describes what data Gull Bomber ("the App") collects and how
it's used.

## What we collect

**Nothing leaves your device.** Gull Bomber currently stores all game data —
your best score, coins earned, unlocked skins, and your selected settings —
locally on your device only, using standard Android app storage. This data
is never transmitted to us or any third party, and we have no server that
receives it.

The App does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or
any other personal information. There is no account creation, no sign-in,
and no analytics tracking of any kind in the current version.

## Advertising

The current version of Gull Bomber does not display ads and does not
include any advertising SDK.

## Third-party services

The current version of Gull Bomber does not integrate any third-party data
or analytics services.

## Children's privacy

Gull Bomber does not knowingly collect any personal information from
anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future update adds features that change what data is collected (for
example, an online leaderboard requiring Google Play Games sign-in, or
advertising), this policy will be updated first, and the update will
describe clearly what's collected and why.
